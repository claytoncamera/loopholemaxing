Ship Log

What shipped recently

The systems above aren't finished products sitting still — they get worked on every week. This is the last 30 days, dated. Every line landed on a live system, and the ones where the answer came back negative are here too.

  1. Aug 26, 2026 OrbitRoute

    The resource contract is now modality-aware — CPU/GPU/NPU/FPGA/QPU — instead of GPU-shaped, with two rules enforced in code rather than policy: a request that states nothing stays GPU-only, so new hardware can never silently capture existing traffic, and capability is evidence-gated — a simulator profile is listed but never compute-eligible. Four public read-only endpoints shipped with it, including a deterministic benchmark anyone can run: the reference solver honestly scores 0 wins and 20 ties against the classical baseline, and the page says so.

  2. Aug 25, 2026 OrbitRoute

    Honesty wave on the fleet itself. The public discovered-satellite count was quietly inflated — 27 of 264 rows were objects the catalogue declines to name, and the endpoint now reads the honest figure. The same wave found the fleet cap breached in a way that could never self-heal: a cycle starting at 11,998 ran its full batch and parked prod permanently red at 12,048 — fixed with cap enforcement inside the add loop and reversible soft deletes. Placeholder "TO BE ASSIGNED" catalog names no longer publish as satellite identities. Canary back to green, now 15 checks.

  3. Aug 25, 2026 Practically Natty

    An accuracy pass over every affiliate-facing surface, because you cannot redesign a dashboard while it gives three different answers to "what do I earn?". The public tier ladder advertised a 50% personal discount the engine has never granted — the same page said 30% higher up, and the wrong number was the more generous one. Live copy now matches the engine everywhere, and the six Elite affiliates finally see their real 15%/16% rates instead of a generic figure.

  4. Aug 24, 2026 OrbitRoute

    The satellite catalog query had been stopping at NORAD id 100,000 — and the five-digit catalog was exhausted in July, so every newer object was structurally invisible. Extended to full legal Alpha-5 scope: the census is complete at 32,306 catalog objects, highest identifier 270,449, with a fifteenth canary check that proves full-scope publication on every cycle instead of trusting it once.

  5. Aug 23, 2026 BTC Brain

    The promotion gate cleared for the first time: the guard24 24-hour candidate reached 190 resolved forecasts at a 65.8% win rate with a Wilson 95% lower bound of 58.8% — above the bar — and positive expectancy after maker fees, so the signal contract flipped from shadow to actionable_paper. Same scoreboard, same rules that said no for months. Still paper-only, still zero trades: manual promotion review is the next gate, and the global ledger remains behind always-guessing-up.

  6. Aug 22, 2026 Practically Natty

    The promotion engine's second full run armed itself the night before and opened on schedule at midnight: the Back to School Sale, up to 30% off through Sep 7, tiers assigned by all-time units sold and generated from live order data by script rather than hand-picked. It registers its own fee-label prefix and purges cache at both ends — the two traps the Summer Sale taught. Same wave, CC2 closed the self-referral loophole: an affiliate buying through their own link no longer earns commission on their own purchase.

  7. Aug 21, 2026 Practically Natty

    A $374 affiliate order went out the door for $47.51 — her own coupon, the 30% personal discount, and peptide credits all stacked, for a true P&L of minus $100.01. Shipped a 50% total-discount ceiling across the whole money path: every discount source still applies, credits calculate last and absorb the clamp, so no combination of promotions can ever discount an order past half price.

  8. Aug 18, 2026 Practically Natty

    Ten dictated storefront edits shipped live: two stacks renamed with 301s, savings rendered red sitewide, checkout instructions moved onto a high-contrast panel, the footer gmail replaced with the brand address, and an unverifiable US-manufacturing claim deleted. Two of the edits briefly took the site down — both my own mistakes, both reverted from backup within a minute, both on the record.

  9. Aug 17, 2026 Practically Natty

    Rebuilt the research stacks from the order book. Nine stacks had sold exactly one unit ever — 0.4% of all-time revenue — and market-basket analysis showed why: five of nine recipes contained a SKU nobody buys, and none included bacteriostatic water, which attaches to 15.6% of orders on its own.
