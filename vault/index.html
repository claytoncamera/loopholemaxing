PersonalVault

by loopholemaxing
Archived · shelved Oct 7, 2026

This prototype is offline

PersonalVault explored a personal document vault with AES-256-GCM encryption in the browser. Its backend was shelved on October 7, 2026, so sign-in and new accounts are closed. Nothing entered here is stored.

  • Documents were encrypted in the browser before upload, with keys derived from the account password using PBKDF2.
  • Six document categories, with encrypted storage behind an API.
  • Security depended on the implementation, the device and key handling; it was never independently audited.
