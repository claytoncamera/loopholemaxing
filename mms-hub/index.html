The system

One path from idea to capital.

The product is not a pile of bots. It is the promotion pipeline around them. Strategies share evidence rules, cost assumptions, hard caps, and one source-of-truth ledger so weak results cannot hide behind a different dashboard.

01

Market data

Real exchange candles and venue data. Synthetic fallback is fail-closed and visibly labeled.

02

Strategy lab

Regime, momentum, arbitrage, catalyst, mean-reversion, and forecast candidates.

03

Risk gate

Capital limits, position caps, daily-loss stops, drawdown breakers, and kill switches.

04

Paper ledger

Timestamped trades, fees, slippage, fills, equity, and comparable forward evidence.

05

Promote or kill

No live path until sample size, confidence, net expectancy, and manual approval all pass.

Promotion gate

Every condition must pass.

This is the line between research and capital. A red gate stops the whole chain; no strategy gets to substitute a backtest, a different period, or a friendlier dashboard.

Real data

No silent synthetic candles or unlabeled fallback.

Costs included

Fees, spread, slippage, and real fill quality.

Sample credible

Enough independent trades and regimes for confidence.

Risk enforced

Per-trade stop, daily loss, drawdown, and kill switch.

Human approval

Promotion remains a deliberate owner decision.

What this preview proves

  • The system records negative findings instead of deleting them.
  • The live BTC ledger can be independently inspected.
  • Execution is separated from research and locked by default.

What it does not claim

  • No strategy is represented as profitable or investor-ready.
  • Backtests are not presented as live returns.
  • No return, win rate, or trade frequency is guaranteed.
