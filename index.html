Four public read-only endpoints shipped with it, including a deterministic benchmark anyone can run: the reference solver honestly scores 0 wins and 20 ties against the classical baseline, and the page says so.

Placeholder "TO BE ASSIGNED" catalog names no longer publish as satellite identities. Canary back to green, now 15 checks.

  10. Aug 25, 2026 Practically Natty

    An accuracy pass over every affiliate-facing surface, because you cannot redesign a dashboard while it gives three different answers to "what do I earn?". Live copy now matches the engine everywhere, and the six Elite affiliates finally see their real 15%/16% rates instead of a generic figure.

Nobody complains about this one, because the people it affects never know they didn't apply. Dropped submissions are now logged instead of vanishing. Same wave: the post-submit view was scrolling away from the "save this number" step on every converting lead, and the Google Jobs markup was violating Google's own HTML-format rules site-wide. 2,018 tests green.

  22. Aug 8, 2026 Work With Clayton

Caught by diffing the deployed HTML against the repository rather than trusting the commit. All pages now match byte-for-byte.   23. Aug 7, 2026 Practically Natty

    Retired the old cartoon mascot from the entire catalogue — 95 images, and zero products, variations or categories still carry it. So the vial was kept and only the label replaced — the glass, cap, lighting and reflections in the existing renders carry no branding, only the label does. The two elements that are the mark were cut as real pixels from the source. Nothing was redrawn by a model.

Published the negative result rather than the backtest that would have read better.
